Getting Around Brunei

Getting around the Abode of Peace is easy, with a variety of convenient transport options to help you explore Brunei at your own pace.

🚘 Dart

Dart is Brunei's local transport app, similar to Uber or Grab. Book a ride conveniently through the app!

Download Dart on Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

🚕 Taxi

Official taxis can be found at major hotels, the Brunei International Airport, and designated taxi stands around Bandar Seri Begawan. Hotel staff can often assist with booking a taxi for you.

🚏 Bus

Brunei's public bus system provides a practical way to traverse Bandar Seri Begawan and its immediate surroundings. While routes can be less frequent outside peak hours, it’s a reliable option for many key destinations. Visit Brunei's Land Transport Department's Website to view bus routes.
